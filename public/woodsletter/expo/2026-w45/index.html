Top story Deal

Shanghai Bohwa and Chengdu Haiming to co-locate two shows across 100,000 m² in 2027

The two organisers signed a strategic partnership in Chengdu on 5 November. HOTELEX Chengdu and the Haiming Chengdu Catering Supply Chain Expo will run together across the whole Chengdu Century City New International Convention and Exhibition Center on 24 to 26 June 2027, with more than 1,500 exhibitors expected.

New shows and changes

01

Automechanika Riyadh moves to June 2027 and a new venue, RFECC Change

Messe Frankfurt Saudi Arabia rescheduled Automechanika Riyadh to 21 to 23 June 2027 at the Riyadh Front Exhibition & Conference Center. The organiser cited recent conditions in Saudi Arabia and safety, and said the larger venue can host more exhibitors.

02

New kitchen and bathroom show KBX to launch in London in September 2027 Launch

KBX, set up by teams from The Assembly and trade title designerati, will debut at London's Business Design Centre on 15 to 17 September 2027. Founding partners include Blanco, Haier, Miele, Quooker and Roman Showers, and invited brands sign 12-month programmes rather than buying stand space only.

03

CIIE 2026 reshapes its halls, grouping the full semiconductor chain in one area Change

The 9th China International Import Expo, which opened on 5 November, kept its six-plus-one layout while enlarging the medical, consumer goods and agrifood areas and grouping the whole semiconductor chain in the technology hall. Free stand space for least developed countries grew 17%, and a new area shows about 100 national brands.

Partnerships and deals

04

Clarion signs multi-year Olympia London deal for four shows through 2028 Deal

The agreement covers The Baby Show, Spirit of Christmas Fair, Destinations and Top Drawer. Signed in the summer and confirmed on 6 November, it extends a partnership of more than a decade; the four shows bring over 150,000 visits to Olympia each year.

05

RX signs MoU with Exhibition World Bahrain to explore shows in Bahrain Deal

Exhibition World Bahrain, the Bahrain Tourism and Exhibitions Authority and RX signed a memorandum of understanding on 5 November during the UFI Global Congress. It is a statement of intent and names no specific shows, dates or scale.

06

Comexposium Middle East signs non-binding letter of intent with Exhibition World Bahrain Deal

The non-binding letter sets a framework for assessing which Comexposium shows could come to Bahrain over the coming years. It was signed during the UFI Global Congress by venue general manager Aage Hansen and Comexposium KSA and Middle East general manager Jawad El Hajri.

AI and digital

07

Meorient launches an export-sales AI agent and discloses stakes in Moonshot AI and StepFun Launch

Meorient unveiled its Xiaoyuan AI agent at the 5th Global Digital Trade Expo, covering market research, buyer outreach, content and show conversion in one chat window. It also said a subsidiary's AI video product has signed over 120 clients and that it is building a vertical model with StepFun.

Industry reports

08

CIIE spillover index report: Hongqiao index up 54.58% year on year Research

The Shanghai Huaxia Institute of Economic Development released its 2026 report on 6 November, measuring six dimensions including international procurement and investment promotion. The national index stood at 62.94 (up 10.27%), Shanghai at 68.19 (up 18.88%) and the Hongqiao business district at 93.01 (up 54.58%).
