Top story Launch

Informa's first Hongqiao tech week draws over 50,000 registered visitors

The event is the China debut of Informa's global tech festival brand and ran from 20 to 22 November at the National Exhibition and Convention Center in Shanghai. It featured six themed stages and more than 200 sessions, with registrations from 156 countries and regions and more than 50,000 registered trade visitors.

New shows and changes

01

Hannover Milano and machinery association launch a Suzhou management congress Launch

The congress and a co-located management services expo both ran for the first time, from 18 to 20 November at the Jinji Lake International Convention Center in Suzhou. The expo had three zones, and the programme included the release of a report on China's top 500 machinery firms.

Messe Graz ends its 120-year autumn fair as the spring fair also stops Change

The organiser said the October anniversary edition was the last and the previously paused spring fair will not return either, with a shift to specialised trade fairs and smaller niche events. Local media reported the decision coincided with a change in venue management.

Bolzano's Good Life debuts as replacement for a 78-year-old autumn fair Launch

The show opened on 13 November for three days, with its first evening running to 22:00, on quality of life, health and conscious consumption. It carries on the Biolife and 78-year-old autumn fair brands, and hosts the 11th South Tyrol volunteer organisations fair during its run.

Going abroad

08

HOMELIFE Vietnam show lands in Hanoi for the first time Launch

The sixth HOMELIFE Vietnam home and gift show, run alongside a Chinese export brands exhibition, took place in Hanoi from 18 to 20 November, the series' first edition in northern Vietnam. Exhibits spanned home gifts, building materials and furniture, machinery and consumer electronics; the organiser said the show has backing from Vietnam's finance ministry and VCCI.

11

Messe Frankfurt takes Texpertise year-round, reaching 60,000 users a month Change

The platform previously carried content mainly around show dates and now runs year-round with industry content, expert interviews, market insight and digital promotion slots, reaching more than 60,000 users a month. Texpertise sits within the group's textile division and is linked to its physical shows.
