New shows and changes

01

London foodservice show FSI moves to Olympia on a three-year deal Change

The three-year agreement starts with a 2027 edition on 28 and 29 September in two main halls. The event comprises nine co-located shows and draws more than 15,000 industry decision makers and 500 exhibitors a year; it leaves a venue used for 16 years for one fresh from a 1.3 billion pound refit.

02

Riyadh aftermarket show moves to 21-23 June 2027 and a new venue Change

The organiser rescheduled the show to 21 to 23 June 2027 and moved it to the Riyadh Front Exhibition and Conference Center. It cited an assessment of recent conditions in the country and safety, and said the new venue has more capacity. The original dates were not disclosed.

03

UK pest control show PestTech moves venue under a six-year deal Change

The association signed a six-year deal running to 2032, with the first edition at the new venue on 2 December 2027. Recent editions drew more than 1,400 visitors and over 85 exhibitors; the association said the move is about room to grow exhibitor numbers and add new experiences.

Good Life debuts as replacement for a fair held for 78 years Launch

The new show opened on 13 November for three days, with its first evening running to 22:00, under a theme of quality of life, health and conscious consumption. It carries on two existing brands, one an autumn fair held for 78 years, and hosts the 11th volunteer organisations fair during its run.
