FFE APAC puts post-harvest tech and cold chain on the floor

At Singapore in November 2027, Fresh Food Expo Asia Pacific splits handling, cooling, packaging and cold chain into two dedicated pillars so technology suppliers meet growers and buyers.

2026-10-23 freshfoodexpoapac.com

Fresh Food Expo Asia Pacific (FFE APAC) divides the value chain into fresh food, agri and fresh technology, and logistics and distribution. The two technology pillars map the journey after harvest: handling, cooling, preservation, storage and packaging, followed by transport, cold chain, warehousing and supply chain management. Organisers argue that shelf life and quality are decided along this technical chain, which is why technology providers sit alongside producers and buyers. The show runs from 16 to 18 November 2027 at the Sands Expo & Convention Centre in Singapore.

Technology scope

Handling and preservation
Solutions for handling, cooling, preservation and storage that cut losses and extend usable life.
Packaging
Packaging technology and materials that protect quality and suit cold chain and retail formats.
Cold chain and warehousing
Transport, cold storage, warehouse management and supply chain systems that keep produce in temperature over long distances.

Why technology suppliers attend

  1. Visitors include wholesalers, importers, exporters, retailers and food service operators who buy technology directly
  2. Exhibitors include producers, packaging companies and cold chain operators looking for complementary links
  3. The show spans Asia Pacific and global markets, a fit for suppliers using Singapore as a base for Southeast Asia
