AgTech China 2026 puts irrigation, greenhouses and smart farming on one floor

The agri-tech wing of China's CAC show reports 160,000 sqm of floor space, more than 100,000 visits and 135 countries, spanning irrigation, greenhouses, spray drones and smart farming.

2026-11-02 一二三互联chinaagtech.com

AgTech China 2026 is the agricultural technology wing of CAC, one of China's large agriculture exhibitions. The official page reports 160,000 sqm of exhibition space, more than 100,000 visits and participation from 135 countries and regions, covering irrigation, greenhouses, agricultural drones, seed, smart farming technology and machinery. Visitors include agricultural cooperatives and corporations, importers, distributors and industry entrants.

Numbers and market context

The official page gives two slightly different sets of figures: the overview says 2,230+ exhibitors from 135 countries and regions, while the body text says 2,320 exhibitors from 130 countries and regions. Both are kept as published, without merging.

The page also notes that China ranks first in the world in output of cereals, cotton, fruit and vegetables, that arable land is limited for its size while total farm output still grows, and that food imports rose sharply over the past decade, making China the world's largest importer of agricultural products.
