First FRESHTech Innovation Award opens for entries, deadline 31 July

Asia Fruit Logistica's FRESHTech platform invites produce technology companies worldwide to submit deployed solutions; the top three will pitch live in Hong Kong on 3 September.

2026-07-07 mp.weixin.qq.com

FRESHTech, the fresh produce technology platform of Asia Fruit Logistica, has launched the first FRESHTech Innovation Award. It is open to produce technology companies worldwide with breakthrough technologies and solutions that could reshape the fresh produce value chain. The award looks beyond novelty: it favours innovation that already works in real industry settings and solves real business problems.

Entry deadline: 31 July 2026
Nominations will be shortlisted in mid August, and results announced live at the FRESHTech Stage in Hong Kong on 3 September.

Who can enter

  1. Launched after January 2025, or already in commercial use
  2. At least one real-world application case
  3. Up to three entries per applicant
  4. Any field is welcome: ripeness detection, cold chain, postharvest, supply chain visibility, AI, quality grading, smart farming and more
