FRESHTech Stage agenda: six themes across the produce tech value chain

On 2 and 3 September, more than 20 speakers at Asia Fruit Logistica cover deployed technology from breeding and sorting to freshness, cold chain and traceability, with the first FRESHTech Innovation Award decided on stage.

2026-08-20 mp.weixin.qq.com

FRESHTech makes its debut at Asia Fruit Logistica 2026. The two-day FRESHTech Stage programme follows the value chain “from field to port, from port to plate”: six themes and more than 20 industry experts and technology practitioners show how technology is moving the produce industry forward. The ten finalists of the first FRESHTech Innovation Award were announced at the same time.
