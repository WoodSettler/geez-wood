Top story In market

Aksu apples reach Beijing shelves in 72 hours on a 0-3 °C chain

Aksu apples are picked, graded, sugar-tested and pre-cooled to 3 °C on the day of harvest, then run 48 hours by 23-tonne reefer to the Shouheng Gaobeidian market for a second sort. Fruit reached a Fangshan supermarket shelf in Beijing on 16 November, 72 hours after leaving the orchard; one trader buys 2,500 to 3,000 tonnes a season.

Why it matters: It compresses pre-cooling, a 48-hour trunk run, a second sort and shelf delivery into one 72-hour window, and a single trader already moves 2,500 to 3,000 tonnes a season.

Breeding and growing

01

Vertical farm brings lettuce to harvest in just over 20 days, not 60 In market

Wuhan Plant Ark's compact vertical farm holds temperature and humidity steady with AI and an environmental control model, adjusting water, fertiliser, light and gas in real time. Lettuce harvests in just over 20 days against about 60 in the field, with no pesticide sprays; the system has sold since 2024 and runs in Hubei, Xinjiang, Inner Mongolia and Zhejiang.

Wuhan Plant Ark Intelligent TechnologyChina新华社 (Xinhua) via 搜狐 ↗
02

Microbial agents lift tomato soluble solids 13.4%, up to 20% at best Trial

A Zhejiang programme led by the provincial academy of agricultural sciences, with the national vegetable system's Hangzhou station, Ningbo's extension station and Zhejiang Wanli University, matched tomato varieties with microbial agents at a field day in Zhenhai on 18 November. Soluble solids rose 13.4% on average and 20% at best, with lower chemical fertiliser use claimed.

National Bulk Vegetable Industry Technology System, Hangzhou comprehensive test station · Ningbo Agricultural Technology Extension Station · Zhejiang Academy of Agricultural Sciences +1China央广网 (CNR) ↗
03

Hong Kong kumquat yields a first-generation orange berry that fruits in a year Research

Huazhong Agricultural University presented a first-generation orange berry bred from the wild Hong Kong kumquat. Ordinary citrus needs at least eight years from sowing to fruit, while the kumquat fruits within one year; about 70 trees fit a mu of orchard, while more than 100 dwarf shrubs fit one greenhouse. The fruit is seedless and free of oil glands.

National Key Laboratory for Germplasm Innovation and Utilization of Horticultural CropsChina极目新闻 (Jimu News) via 搜狐 ↗

Harvesting and automation

04

Orchard robot picks a fruit in 10 seconds, climbs 35° with 200 kg Demo

Hubei University of Technology's agricultural machinery institute showed a fully automatic harvesting robot. It recognises apples, pears and navel oranges and places fruit in an onboard bin at about 10 seconds each, with LiDAR for obstacle avoidance; the tracked chassis climbs 35 degrees and carries 200 kilograms, and the team says it is in demonstration use in Wuhan.

Hubei University of Technology, Agricultural Machinery Engineering Research and Design InstituteChina新华社 (Xinhua) via 搜狐 ↗

Grading, inspection and data

05

Chilean cherry packhouses add optical sorting; 87% of exports go to China In market

In O'Higgins and Curicó, cherries are water-cooled and pre-cooled before optical sorting, then stacked by palletising robots. Micro-perforated atmosphere bags adjust oxygen and carbon dioxide inside the pack to inhibit microbes and prevent condensation. Ministry data put 2025/26 exports at about 569,000 tonnes, 87% to China.

Chilean Ministry of Agriculture · Chilean Cherry Committee · Chilean Fruit Exporters Association +1Chile · China中原网 (Zhongyuan Net) ↗新华社 (Xinhua) via 搜狐 ↗
06

Shenzhen digital twin reaches 750 cold-chain operators in 30 provinces In market

Shenzhen Puleng runs 40 cold stores in 26 cities, with remote temperature monitoring and GPS on its vehicles. Qianhai Yueshi's digital twin merges warehousing, transport and customs data for full-chain traceability and temperature visibility, deployed with more than 750 cold-chain operators across 30 provinces.

Postharvest and packaging

07

Fyffes banana pack cuts plastic 46%, about 16 kg per container Trial

Fyffes' new banana packing system uses 46% less plastic per container, about 16 kilograms, while keeping fruit protected and fresh. The company is also adding polypack packaging with recycled content and a paper banana band without plastic backing, and developing compostable cluster bags and plastic-free pineapple labelling.

FyffesEurope · Latin AmericaFruitnet (Eurofruit) ↗
08

Tea-polyphenol coating keeps fresh-cut apples pale for 16 days at 4 °C Research

A Food Chemistry study built an edible coating from chitosan, L-cysteine, tea polyphenols and tea-polyphenol-loaded chitosan nanoparticles. Over 16 days at 4 °C, the four-component coating beat water-dipped controls on browning, weight loss, reactive oxygen species, polyphenol oxidase and peroxidase activity and microbial growth, with higher antioxidant enzyme activity.
