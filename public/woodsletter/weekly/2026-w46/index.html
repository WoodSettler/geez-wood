Top story In market

Cold-chain train to Chongqing goes regular: 650 tonnes, cost down 15%

A train carrying 650 tonnes of Lao bananas and Yunnan plateau vegetables left Kunming on 12 November and reached Chongqing 31 hours later, starting regular service. Imported fruit and local leafy vegetables now share one consist, the operator reports about 15% lower logistics cost, and two to three trains a week are planned.

Why it matters: It is the first regular service that groups imported fruit with local vegetables, and the cost claim now comes with a number and a schedule.

Breeding and growing

01

Heat-tolerant strawberries bred in 3.5 years, fruiting in 6 weeks indoors In market

Xinguo used flowering genes to target crosses and brought a heat-tolerant variety to market in three and a half years, against more than a decade conventionally. In its Guangzhou plant factory, simulated autumn then shorter days and cooler nights bring fruit in as little as six weeks.

Sino-Singapore Guangzhou Knowledge City · Xinguo AgricultureChina · Singapore广州日报 (Guangzhou Daily) ↗

Grading, inspection and data

02

Yantai apple lines grade 10,000 fruit an hour with infrared sugar and defect scans In market

At Qixia Hefu, apples are graded on colour, shape, size and weight, then scanned with infrared light for sugar, firmness and internal defects, with substandard fruit rejected; the line handles over 10,000 fruit an hour. Yantai Customs reports 164,000 tonnes of fresh apple exports, up 5.1%.

Yantai Customs · Qixia Hefu Fruit and Vegetable · Yantai Tacheng Fruit and Vegetable Cold StorageChina网易山东 (NetEase Shandong), citing local reporting ↗
03

Durian line uses CT and AI to grade 13 internal traits at over 98% accuracy Launch

Shiboshi launched Durian Detective 2.0 at the ninth CIIE, upgraded from a single machine to a fully automatic line: CT and AI scan two durians at a time for weight, chambers, flesh yield, insect holes and burnt core, with accuracy above 98% and flesh-yield deviation within 5%.

ShiboshiChina · Malaysia中国青年报 (China Youth Daily) ↗农民日报 (Farmers' Daily) ↗中华工商时报 (China Business Times) ↗
04

Korla pear line sorts 60 tonnes a day, with 7,000 tonnes of cold storage behind it In market

Shayidong has put a domestic LüMeng sorting line into service at 60 tonnes a day: 360-degree imaging for shape, weighing to plus or minus one gram, and near-infrared sugar testing, while screening colour, puncture damage and hollow core. Sorted pears keep eight to nine months at 7,000 tonnes of cold store.

Shayidong Korla Fragrant Pear Industry DevelopmentChina农民日报 (Farmers' Daily) ↗

Postharvest and packaging

05

Electron-beam treatment keeps figs firm 20 days refrigerated and 3 days ambient In market

A Yantai University team led by Yang Lei treats fresh figs with high-energy electron beams instead of cobalt-60 gamma rays, leaving no residue when the machine stops. Controlled dosing slows respiration and ripening and kills moulds and pests; fruit holds three days at room temperature and 20 days refrigerated.

Yantai University, School of Nuclear Equipment and Nuclear Engineering · Partner fig growerChina大众日报 (Dazhong Daily) ↗
06

Ventilated wrap cuts pallet packaging from 2.5 kg to 180 g and waste by about 90% In market

Tama's InnoVent replaces corner boards and strapping with a stretchable single-material polyethylene wrap that holds the load and still ventilates. The company reports about 90% less packaging waste, up to 30% lower cost and an 88% smaller carbon footprint; each wrap weighs 180 grams against about 2.5 kilograms.

Tama GroupIsrael · United States · EuropeFreshPlaza ↗
07

Review puts numbers on functional packaging: grapes lose 15.5% in 18 days, not 40.4% Research

A review from the South China Botanical Garden maps moisture and gas regulation, edible coatings, antimicrobial and intelligent packaging and shelf-life prediction onto one framework. Cited benchmarks include a chitosan/nanocellulose coating that cut grape weight loss to 15.5% after 18 days against 40.4% in controls.

South China Botanical Garden, Chinese Academy of SciencesChinaChinese Academy of Sciences (English newsroom) ↗
