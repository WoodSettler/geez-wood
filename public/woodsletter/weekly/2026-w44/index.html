Top story In market

Fewer than 1 in 5 durians pass AI screening for CIIE

At the Lingang logistics hub serving exhibitors at the 9th China International Import Expo, candidate durians are checked by hand and then graded by an AI non-destructive tester for maturity, chamber count and flesh yield. Fewer than 20% clear all three thresholds.

Why it matters: Here AI testing replaces cutting fruit open and decides whether premium imports reach the show floor and the Shanghai market, a sign the technology has entered day-to-day import operations.

Postharvest and packaging

01

Vidre+ adds 4.95 sellable days to table grapes in nine European trials Trial

Fresh Inset reported nine postharvest trials in Italy, Greece, Spain and the UK. Marketable life rose by 4.95 days on average, about 35% longer than untreated fruit, and rachis browning was delayed by 5.6 days.

Fresh InsetItaly · Greece · Spain · United KingdomThe Produce News ↗
02

Golden Bay drops plastic film on Tesco apple packs, now 10,000 packs a week In market

New Zealand grower Golden Bay Fruit replaced plastic film on its Miranda apple packs at Tesco with a cardboard tray and linerless lid from Ravenwood Packaging. Supply now runs at 10,000 packs a week, and the brand says sales beat expectations.

03

Bagasse-and-shellac insert cuts strawberry surface rot from 82% to 52% Research

Michigan State University's School of Packaging coated bagasse paper with four shellac layers to release ethanol vapour against Botrytis. After five days of sealed storage, inoculated strawberries showed 52% surface spoilage versus 82% in controls, with no significant change in firmness or colour.

Michigan State University School of PackagingUnited StatesPostHarvest.biz ↗
04

AI-designed bio-based wrap keeps avocados fresh for 7 days at room temperature Research

A University of Maryland team combined robotic experiments, machine learning and chemical engineering to make a bio-based wrap with plastic-like strength. Wrapped avocados stayed fresh for seven days at room temperature while unwrapped ones spoiled; the university says shelf life doubles.

University of Maryland, Department of Chemical and Biomolecular EngineeringUnited StatesUniversity of Maryland (Maryland Today) ↗

Harvesting and automation

05

Picking robots multiply: 1,600 grape bunches and 800+ apples an hour Demo

A swappable-head picker at the Yangling fair is rated at 1,600 grape bunches and 1,400 kiwifruit an hour, while a four-arm apple robot from a Beijing-led team picked more than 800 fruit an hour in Laizhou orchards. Both are demonstrations; success rates were not disclosed.

Beijing Academy of Agriculture and Forestry Sciences, Intelligent Equipment Research Center · Shanghai Jiao Tong University · Nongxin Technology +1China大众网 (Dazhong Net), Yantai ↗CCTV Plus (China Central Television) ↗
06

Automatic grafting machine debuts at 1,200 seedlings an hour Launch

Hefei Jiafute Robotics showed a fully automatic fruit and vegetable grafting machine at the Yangling fair. It handles pickup, cutting, splice grafting, wrapping and planting without manual work, at a rated 1,200 seedlings an hour.

Cold chain and logistics

07

Albion cuts fleet claim costs 74% after adding cold-chain telematics In market

UK fresh food distributor Albion Fine Foods deployed Webfleet software and cameras across its refrigerated fleet, streaming cabin temperatures and keeping an auditable record of temperature-controlled deliveries. Fleet claim costs fell 74%.

Albion Fine Foods · Webfleet (Bridgestone)United KingdomFruitnet (Fresh Produce Journal) ↗Motor Transport ↗
08

Highland vegetables pre-cooled to 3°C reach Hong Kong in 72 hours In market

Cool-climate vegetables from about 2,600 metres in Haidong, Qinghai, are pre-cooled, sorted and packed within 18 hours of harvest, then travel more than 2,000 km under continuous cold chain to reach Hong Kong and Guangdong supermarkets in about 72 hours.

Breeding and cultivation

09

Tomato virus-resistance screening drops from 2 to 3 years to 24 hours Deal

Tongzhou seed park licensed Shandong Agricultural University's TBR1 marker test for tomato brown rugose fruit virus resistance. A young-leaf sample returns a result within 24 hours, against two to three years of field phenotyping, cutting the breeding cycle by about two thirds.

Shandong Agricultural University · Tongzhou International Seed Industry Science and Technology ParkChina北京城市副中心报 via 新浪财经 (Beijing Sub-center Daily via Sina Finance) ↗
