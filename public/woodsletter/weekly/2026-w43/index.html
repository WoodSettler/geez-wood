Top story In market

China-Laos rail cold chain hauls 151,000 t of vegetables, up 23%

Yunnan vegetables are graded, pre-cooled and packed, then loaded straight into railway reefer containers for Laos. China Railway Kunming Group runs mixed fresh and general freight with priority for produce; one supplier ships 24 to 25 tonnes a day, rising to 60 to 70 at peak.

Why it matters: Rail cold chain is now a regular corridor for China–Southeast Asia produce trade, and fast volume growth means more demand for grading, pre-cooling and packing at origin.

Sorting, inspection and data

01

AI cells pick pit fragments from 15 t/h of peach halves in Greece In market

Eight QING STAQ inspection cells with Stäubli SCARA robots process up to 15 tonnes of peaches an hour at Greek processor Agrophoenix, about 200,000 halves, rejecting some 3,600 an hour. The system has completed its first harvest season.

QualiCrop tomato grading line launches with 87% defect accuracy Launch

Fotenix multispectral inspection and Leap AI robotic handling detect and remove defective tomatoes on a moving conveyor at commercial packhouse speeds. Innovate UK funded the project with GBP 385,000; the University of Lincoln is a partner.

Three Shizuoka co-ops open an AI citrus sorting centre for 7,500 t a year In market

The 2,668-square-metre centre uses AI vision to inspect surface damage and decay and stacks cartons automatically. JA Shimizu, JA Oigawa and JA Haibara now ship jointly under one brand, Suruga mikan.

UBQ's Fresh IDX turns temperature logs into lost shelf-life days In market

Fresh IDX converts temperature history into days of shelf life lost to temperature abuse, then uses sensory data and biological models to predict mould and pitting before they appear. A separate report says it has analysed more than 3,000 inbound berry shipments.

Postharvest and packaging

05

Freza sachet keeps 5 kg of produce for up to 30 days, no fridge In market

Each sachet costs 1,000 Ugandan shillings and releases a plant-derived gas that slows spoilage. More than 100,000 have been sold to over 11,000 farmers since launch, and a bulk unit for pack houses is now available.

06

Carbon-dot packaging keeps cut leafy greens 15 days without dips or gas Trial

Zhongkai University of Agriculture and Engineering presented the packaging at an industry forum, reporting trial results of 15 days for lettuce and bok choy and 26 days for cabbage. Chives supplied to a restaurant chain lasted seven days at 7 to 9°C despite repeated opening.

07

Liaoning cold-store protocol stretches Dahongpao plum storage past 40 days In market

The Liaoning Institute of Pomology released plum and grape storage protocols that require pre-cooling within 12 hours of harvest. Late grapes can keep for five to seven months, and a Dashiqiao cooperative is holding plums until mid-November.

Breeding and cultivation

08

Pairwise launches Fontana, a compact blackberry half the usual height Launch

Gene-editing company Pairwise released Fontana, a blackberry 3.5 to 5 feet tall, about half the height of standard plants. It needs no pruning and little trellis work, suits high-density planting and fruits earlier; thornless and seedless lines are planned.
