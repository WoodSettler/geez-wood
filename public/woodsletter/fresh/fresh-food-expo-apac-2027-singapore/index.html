Fresh Food Expo Asia Pacific heads to Singapore in November 2027

The new B2B show links fresh produce, agri and freshness technology and logistics in one venue, running 16 to 18 November 2027 at Sands Expo in Singapore.

2026-10-23 freshfoodexpoapac.com

Fresh Food Expo Asia Pacific (FFE APAC) is a B2B platform for the region's fresh food value chain, bringing fresh products, freshness and cold chain technology and logistics under one roof. Organisers position it as a connector from source to market: freshness and availability depend not only on the product but on technology, cold chain infrastructure, logistics networks, packaging, storage and efficient distribution. The show runs from 16 to 18 November 2027 at the Sands Expo & Convention Centre in Singapore.

Three pillars

Fresh Food
Fresh food
Produce from growers, producers, farms, fisheries, exporters and suppliers across Asia Pacific and global markets, the starting point of the chain.
Agri & Fresh Technology
Agri and fresh technology
Technologies and solutions for handling, cooling, preservation, storage and packaging that protect quality and extend shelf life.
Logistics & Distribution
Logistics and distribution
Transport, cold chain, warehousing and supply chain solutions that move fresh food safely and efficiently to buyers and markets.

Who should attend

  1. Visitors: wholesalers, importers, exporters, retailers and food service operators
  2. Exhibitors: fresh food producers, packaging companies and cold chain operators
  3. The show argues that every link in the chain is interdependent, so suppliers, technology providers, logistics operators, distributors and buyers meet in one place

The show is run by Fresh Food Expo Asia Pacific. Details on visiting and exhibiting are available at freshfoodexpoapac.com.
