IFPA 2026 wraps in Orlando with buyers from more markets than ever

The International Fresh Produce Association's annual show drew attendees from nearly 70 countries and more than 1,050 exhibitors, with a 10% rise in non-US retailers; next year it moves to Atlanta.

2026-10-17 Doug Ohlemeierperishablenews.com

The International Fresh Produce Association (IFPA) closed its 2026 Global Produce & Floral Show in Orlando, Florida. For the first time the show ran midweek, Wednesday to Friday, at members' request, and it attracted buyers from more global markets than ever before.

Five themes across the show

  1. Rebuilding consumer trust through food safety, traceability and prevention-focused standards
  2. Protecting year-round supply through trade, labour and smart policy
  3. Sustainability and packaging, including workable EPR approaches and $5 million in grants for the Packaging Innovation Program
  4. Putting AI to work for members, with expanded food safety and consumer insights in the Global Intelligence Engine
  5. Making fruits, vegetables and flowers central to health strategy

On the show floor

The buyer-to-attendee ratio was almost 1:9. Curated tours took buyers straight to solution providers, including the new Fresh Supply Chain of the Future Tour, the Regenerative Agriculture Tour and Ag Attaché tours of key production regions.

The Innovation Hub placed first-time exhibitors and the Fresh Ideas Showcase, with 235 new innovations, next to the Food Safety & Tech X-Change, where experts covered topics from traceability to food safety science.
